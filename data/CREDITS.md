# SayCanto dictionary data: credits and licences

The SayCanto dictionary database (`dictionary.db`) is an adaptation of the sources below and is
distributed under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

**Changes made:** the sources were merged into one database; Cantonese (Jyutping) readings were joined to
CC-CEDICT headwords; overlapping entries were combined; meanings were split and normalised for search;
a word-frequency score and word types (parts of speech) were derived for each entry; hand-written SayCanto
entries replace source entries with the same characters and reading.
